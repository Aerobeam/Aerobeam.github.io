What we do.

AeroBeam develops drone deployed laser cleaning, laser analysis, and imaging tools that remove surface contamination quickly and precisely, with minimal downtime. AeroBeam's foundational PhD research was funded by ESB and Sustainable Energy Authority Ireland. Our first route to market is handheld ground based wind turbine blade inspection and then moving to provide targeted cleaning to recover lost energy yield.
